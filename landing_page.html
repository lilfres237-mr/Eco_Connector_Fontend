Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using EcoConnect.

How do I request a waste pickup?

In the app, choose Create Request and enter your address, waste type, estimated weight, contact number, and preferred pickup date. Confirm to submit it.

Can I choose where my pickup happens?

Enter your pickup address directly. The optional map picker requires a Google Maps key to be configured in the app.

How can I become a rider?

Create an account, then open Profile and select “Earn as a Rider.” The registration flow requests identification and vehicle details.

Which languages are available?

The app currently includes English and French. Change the language in Settings.

How do I get help with a specific request?

Use the support chat on this page for general app guidance. For account or pickup specific assistance, contact the support team through the in-app Help Center or Messages.
